# Customer Order Form History

> **new and updated** on November 16, 2026

Diamond Comic Distributors first launched the PREVIEWSworld website in May 2009 to replace its older consumer-facing site (PREVIEWSonline.com) and host downloadable digital Customer Order Forms. However, Diamond began offering downloadable plain-text (orderform.txt) and early PDF versions of the PREVIEWS Customer Order Form years earlier—at least as far back as July 2002 on diamondcomics.com.

* Print Origins (January 1989): Diamond launched the PREVIEWS catalog in January 1989, originally bundling a separate printed Customer Order Form booklet inside or alongside the catalog for readers to hand to their local shop.
* Early Digital Text & PDF Forms (Late 1990s – July 2002): Before PREVIEWSworld existed, Diamond hosted downloadable Customer Order Forms on its main corporate site (diamondcomics.com) and early portal (previewsonline.com). Archival records of Diamond's digital Customer Order Form files on diamondcomics.com date back to July 2002, when plain-text (.txt) checklists were standard for dial-up users and early automated pull-list scripts, alongside early PDF layouts.
* PREVIEWSworld Launch (May 2009): In May 2009, Diamond consolidated its consumer solicitations, item codes, and monthly downloadable .txt and .pdf Customer Order Forms onto PREVIEWSworld.com, which remained the standard monthly download hub until the catalog went digital-only in June 2025 (for the July 2025 issue) and subsequently shut down

# Previews World Decommissioned

Diamond Comics Distributors filed for bankruptcy in early 2025. They briefly supported a digital only PREVIEWS Catalog in mid-2025. By late 2025 and early 2026, Diamond and its remaining corporate offices closed permanently. Visiting previewsworld.com now results in dead links, 404 errors, or redirects to affiliated corporate sites like Enesco.
