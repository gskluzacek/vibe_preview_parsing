# Previews World Decommissioned

Diamond Comics Distributors filed for bankruptcy in early 2025. They briefly supported a digital only PREVIEWS Catalog in mid-2025. By late 2025 and early 2026, Diamond and its remaining corporate offices closed permanently. Visiting previewsworld.com now results in dead links, 404 errors, or redirects to affiliated corporate sites like Enesco.

# Replacement Distributor websites

see: https://share.google/aimode/erevtOkVq4za7e6Pz

* Penguin Random House (PRH): Handles Marvel Comics, Dark Horse Comics, IDW Publishing, BOOM! Studios, and Oni Press. You can browse upcoming releases and monthly previews on the PRH Comics Retail Portal.
* https://prhcomics.com
* Lunar Distribution: Handles DC Comics, Image Comics, Dynamite Entertainment, Titan Comics, Vault Comics, and Mad Cave Studios. Monthly digital catalogs like DC Connect and Image Scene are posted directly on the Lunar Distribution Website.
* https://www.lunardistribution.com

# alternative resources

see: https://share.google/aimode/XOMLZctq5rl3QWgnx

* Comic Releases Master Catalog: Browse a unified, filterable database of all upcoming single issues, cover variants, and Final Order Cutoff (FOC) dates across every publisher.
* https://www.comicreleases.com
* League of Comic Geeks: The closest all-in-one replacement for PREVIEWSworld's customer order form. You can browse upcoming releases months in advance, filter by publisher or Final Order Cutoff (FOC) date, check variant covers, and export or sync your pull list directly with participating local comic shops
* https://leagueofcomicgeeks.com
