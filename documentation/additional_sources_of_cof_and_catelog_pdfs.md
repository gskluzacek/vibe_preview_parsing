# Wayback Machine's URL search box
https://web.archive.org
* 2000s–2010s diamondcomics.com Path: Diamond stored plain-text and PDF forms in a predictable archive folder using a MMMYY_COF naming convention (for example, http://www.diamondcomics.com/support/previews_docs/orderforms/archive/2010/APR10_COF.txt). Entering diamondcomics.com/support/previews_docs/orderforms/* into the Wayback Machine's URL search box will list all captured .txt and .pdf files in that directory.
* previewsworld.com Path: Later monthly text files and PDFs were hosted under ://previewsworld.com* or linked directly from ://previewsworld.com* solicitation announcement pages


# Retailer & Third-Party Archives
Several comic shops and document platforms still host copies of older order forms on their own servers:

## Alternate Worlds
* Alternate Worlds (2024–2025): This Australian retailer maintains a public Preorder Catalogues Page that still links to downloadable Previews plain-text (.txt) order files and publisher PDFs from late 2024 and early 2025.
* https://alternateworlds.com.au/preorder-catalogues/

## ACE Comics
* ACE Comics (CSV Archive): For spreadsheet-formatted lists, the UK shop ACE Comics keeps a multi-year archive of downloadable .csv advance order forms organized by catalog volume.
* https://acecomics.co.uk/customer-order-forms/

# YUMPU / Mile High Comics - PDFs
* YUMPU & Retailer PDFs: Individual PDF copies from the early 2010s through 2020s are scattered across document hosts like YUMPU and shop servers like Mile High Comics. Searching "THE PREVIEWS CUSTOMER ORDER FORM" filetype:pdf on Google will surface remaining hosted PDFs
* https://www.yumpu.com/en/document/view/24506636/2-2013-pdf-previewsworld
* https://www.milehighcomics.com/html/10_20_cof.pdf
